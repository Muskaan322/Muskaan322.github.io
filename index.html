福井 Fukui, Japan · Sep – Dec 2026

Visiting Research Fellow, Japan

The Government of Japan invited Muskaan to the University of Fukui as a visiting research fellow. The program runs from September to December 2026. It included 21 days of in-person research from September 20 to October 10, with field visits to Fukui and the Rainbow Line, Kanazawa and Omicho Market in Ishikawa, and Osaka. There she worked on AI-driven tourism research with the Distributed Human Data Engine (DHDE).

  • Combined AI-camera visitor counts, Japan Meteorological Agency weather data, route-search signals and tourism surveys.
  • Studied international visitor demand in Fukui and how weather keeps tourists away.
  • Used forecasting, statistical modelling and spatial analysis to find openings for regional tourism.
  • Earned a Certificate of Achievement for the program and a Certificate of Appreciation from the University of Fukui.

She also gave a talk, Tourism AI: Learn with the World, on Fukui's tourism through Pakistani eyes and what goes on behind a real AI project. Japan's Tannan FM Magazine featured her contribution to the research.
