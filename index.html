福井 Fukui, Japan · 2026

Invited to Japan as a Sakura Science researcher

The Government of Japan invited Muskaan to the University of Fukui under the JST Sakura Science Program. There she worked on AI-driven tourism research with the Distributed Human Data Engine (DHDE).

  • Combined AI-camera visitor counts, Japan Meteorological Agency weather data, route-search signals and tourism surveys.
  • Studied international visitor demand in Fukui and how weather keeps tourists away.
  • Used forecasting, statistical modelling and spatial analysis to find openings for regional tourism.

She also gave a talk, Tourism AI: Learn with the World, on Fukui's tourism through Pakistani eyes and what goes on behind a real AI project. Japan's Tannan FM Magazine featured her contribution to the research.
